Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Guidelines (HR-CORP-2026)

Section 1: Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Accrual
Full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off at a rate of 1.67 days per month, yielding 20 standard days per calendar year. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year; unused days beyond this limit expire on December 31.

Section 2: Parental Leave Entitlement
Under standard HR-POL-101, all full-time primary caregivers are entitled to 16 consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 consecutive weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.

Section 3: Remote Work Policy
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with formal managerial approval. Remote team members must maintain core working hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time and ensure an ergonomically sound home office.

Section 4: Grievance Escalation Procedure
Any workplace harassment or ethical grievance should be formally submitted to People Operations within 14 calendar days of the incident. All investigations are strictly confidential and concluded within 30 days.
