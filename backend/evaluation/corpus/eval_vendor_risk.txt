Third-Party Vendor Risk & Compliance Standards (VND-RISK-2026)

Section 1: Vendor Criticality Classification
Vendors are categorized into three criticality tiers. Classification VND-TIER-1 applies to any third-party provider that stores, processes, or transmits confidential customer data, or operates critical cloud hosting infrastructure.

Section 2: Mandatory Security Certifications
Pursuant to compliance requirement POL-402, all VND-TIER-1 vendors must provide an annual SOC 2 Type II audit report and maintain an active ISO 27001 certification. Failure to provide certifications results in immediate contract suspension.

Section 3: Commercial Liability & Indemnification
Standard enterprise master vendor agreements mandate a minimum commercial general liability coverage of $5,000,000 per occurrence. Cyber liability insurance must cover a minimum of $10,000,000.

Section 4: Termination & Cryptographic Offboarding
Upon termination of vendor engagement, all cryptographic keys, service account credentials, and federated SSO bindings must be permanently revoked within 4 hours. The vendor must provide a formal Certificate of Data Destruction within 30 days.
