Enterprise Information Security Policy & Data Retention (SEC-POL-2026)

Section 1: Data Classification
All enterprise data must be classified into one of four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer financial data and credentials are classified as Restricted.

Section 2: Access Control & Password Security
Access control adheres to ISO-27001-A.9 standards. Password rotation is strictly enforced every 90 days with a minimum length of 14 characters, requiring alphanumeric and special characters. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all internal and VPN access.

Section 3: Data Retention Limits
Under policy RET-90, customer personally identifiable information (PII) retention is capped at 7 years following account closure. Operational server telemetry and access logs must be retained for exactly 90 days before secure deletion.

Section 4: Incident Escalation Protocol
Under security protocol SEC-8012, any Severity-1 security breach or unauthorized data exfiltration must be escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the Incident Response Team within 2 hours of detection.
